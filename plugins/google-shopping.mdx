The **Google Shopping** plugin runs a Google Shopping search and returns the product cards as structured JSON: title, price (as a number and as Google prints it), currency (the symbol and its ISO 4217 code), the original price and discount when the product is on sale, the merchant, rating and review count, and Google's own ids for the product.

Give it a search term and pick the market with `gl` (and `proxy_country`), because prices and merchants on Google Shopping are region-specific. Filter by a price range in that market's currency. Each call returns the first page of products. Google Shopping has no per-product URL on its result cards, so products come without a `url`; use `productId` instead, which stays the same across searches. A product Google repeats on the page (for example in a topic shelf) is listed once.

## Search

```
POST /goods/google-shopping
```

| Field | Required | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `q` | yes | Product search term, e.g. `wireless headphones`. |
| `gl` | no | Market to search, ISO-2, e.g. `us` or `de`. Sets the prices and merchants. Default `us`. |
| `min_price` / `max_price` | no | Price range in whole units of the `gl` market's currency. |
| `max_results` | no | How many products to return from the first page, `1`-`100`. Default `60`. |
| `proxy_country` | no | Proxy country (ISO-2). Prices and merchants are market-specific, so match it to `gl`. |

```bash cURL
curl -X POST "https://api.scrapeunblocker.com/goods/google-shopping?q=wireless%20headphones&gl=us&max_results=3" \

```

A `403` means Google kept answering with a CAPTCHA; retry, or try another `proxy_country`. A `502` means no products could be collected (no matches, or the call did not finish in time).

| Field | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| `title` | Product title as Google shows it. |
| `price` / `priceRaw` | Numeric price and the price text as Google prints it. |
| `currency` / `currencyCode` | The currency as Google displays it on the card (e.g. `$`), and its ISO 4217 code (`USD`). For a symbol several currencies share (`$`, `¥`, `kr`), the code follows the market (`gl`): `$` is `CAD` with `gl=ca`. |
| `originalPrice` / `discount` | The price before a sale and the discount, when the card shows one. |
| `merchant` | The store selling the product, when Google names one. |
| `rating` / `reviewCount` | Star rating (out of 5) and the review count as Google prints it (a string such as `32K`). |
| `image` | Product image, when available. |
| `productId` / `catalogId` / `offerId` | Google's own ids for the product, its catalog entry and the offer. They stay the same across searches, so use `productId` to follow one product over time or to merge results; the list is already deduplicated by it. |

A field with no value is left out of that product, so a card may carry only `title` and price.

## What you can build

- **Price comparison** - see which merchants sell a product and at what price, per market.
- **Price history** - follow one product by `productId` across daily searches.
- **MAP and reseller monitoring** - track the prices merchants advertise for your brand's products.
- **Market pricing research** - compare price levels for a product type across countries with `gl`.
- **Retail coverage** - combine with the [Amazon](/plugins/amazon) and [Walmart](/plugins/walmart) plugins for a broader price view.
