The **Product Hunt** plugin turns Product Hunt into structured JSON. It has four modes:

- **`leaderboard`** - a day's launches with name, tagline, votes, comments, topics and the daily, weekly and monthly rank. Pass a past `date` for a finalised, ranked board; today's board is live and not ranked yet.
- **`search`** - products matching a keyword.
- **`topic`** - products under a topic slug such as `artificial-intelligence`.
- **`detail`** - one product by slug, with its description, website, social links, review rating, followers, categories and latest launch.

No Product Hunt account or API token is involved.

<Note>
Try it in the dashboard first: [app.scrapeunblocker.com/dashboard/plugins](https://app.scrapeunblocker.com/dashboard/plugins). You get a live results view plus a copy-paste snippet.
</Note>

## Launches, search, topics and products

```
POST /products/producthunt
```

| Field | Required | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `mode` | no | What to fetch: `leaderboard` (default), `search`, `topic` or `detail`. |
| `query` | with `search` | Search terms, e.g. `ai chatbot`. |
| `date` | no | Leaderboard day as `YYYY-MM-DD` (`leaderboard` mode). Omit for today. |
| `topic` | with `topic` | Topic slug, e.g. `artificial-intelligence`. |
| `slug` | with `detail` | Product slug, e.g. `notion`. |
| `max_results` | no | Max products for the list modes (`leaderboard`, `search`, `topic`), `1`-`200`. Default `30`. |
| `proxy_country` | no | Proxy country the request is sent from (ISO-2). Best left blank: Product Hunt is global. |

| Field | Notes |

| `topics` | Topic `id`, `slug` and `name`. |
| `thumbnail` | Product thumbnail URL. |
| `featuredAt` / `createdAt` | When the launch was featured and created. |

## What you can build

- **Launch tracking** - pull each day's ranked leaderboard and keep a history of top launches.
- **Competitor watch** - search your category and get alerted when a new product launches.
- **Market research by topic** - list everything launched under a topic with votes and comments.
- **Lead and partner lists** - enrich products with their website and social links from `detail` mode.
