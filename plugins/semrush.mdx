The **Semrush** plugin returns the data on Semrush's free website pages as structured JSON, with no Semrush account, login or API key. Give it a domain, any URL on the site or a Semrush website page URL, and pick the report:

- **`overview`** (default) - the Authority Score, global, country and category rank with their changes, monthly visits with their desktop and mobile history, pages per visit, time on site and bounce rate, organic and paid search traffic with history, backlinks and referring domains with history and Semrush's Google penalty risk, top organic keywords with position, search volume, CPC, traffic share and intent, top countries, competitors, incoming and outgoing traffic sources, and visits from AI assistants such as ChatGPT and Claude.
- **`competitors`** - the site's competitor list, each competitor with its Authority Score, ranks, visits and engagement.

The figures are Semrush's own estimates, and `dataDate` names the period they describe. Semrush publishes these pages for about 200,000 of the most visited sites; any other domain comes back with `found: false`.

## Website data

```
POST /seo/semrush
```

| Field | Required | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `domain` | yes | Website to look up: a domain (`github.com`), a URL (`https://www.github.com/about`) or a Semrush website page URL. A leading `www.` is dropped; other subdomains are kept. |
| `report` | no | `overview` (default) or `competitors`. |

| Field | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| `found` | `false` when Semrush has no public page for the domain. |
| `dataDate` / `updatedAt` | The period the figures describe and when Semrush last updated the page. |
| `authorityScore` | Semrush Authority Score (0-100). |
| `globalRank` / `countryRank` / `categoryRank` | Ranks with their change; `countryRank` is in the site's top country, `categoryRank` in its main category. |
| `visits` / `visitsChangePercent` / `visitsHistory` | Monthly visits, the change in percent, and the monthly history split into `desktopVisits` and `mobileVisits`. |
| `pagesPerVisit` / `timeOnSiteSeconds` / `bounceRate` | Engagement. `bounceRate` is a 0-1 share. |
| `searchTraffic` | Organic and paid search traffic with their change and history. |
| `backlinks` | Backlinks and referring domains with their change and history, plus `googlePenaltyRisk`. |
| `topKeywords` | Top organic keywords in the site's main market: `position`, `searchVolume`, `cpc`, `trafficShare` and search `intents`. |
| `topCountries` | Countries by visits, with traffic share and the desktop/mobile split. |
| `competitors` | Competing sites with `relevance` and visits. Use `report=competitors` for the full list with ranks and engagement. |
| `trafficSources` | `incoming` sources and `outgoing` destinations, each with share, visits and the previous period's visits. |
| `aiTraffic` | Visits from AI assistants (ChatGPT, Claude and others) with their monthly history. |

## Errors

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `400` | Missing or malformed `domain`, or a `report` other than `overview` / `competitors`. |
| `502` | No Semrush page was returned (blocked or timed out). Retry. |

## What you can build

- **Competitor benchmarking** - compare Authority Score, visits and search traffic across rival sites every month.
- **Prospect research** - enrich a list of domains with traffic, backlinks and top countries before outreach.
- **AI visibility tracking** - follow how many visits a site gets from AI assistants over time.
- **Market maps** - expand from one site to its competitor set with `report=competitors`.
